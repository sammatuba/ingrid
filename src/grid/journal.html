Sources and method

Journal entries are written by signed-in traders and stay private unless shared with the desk.

"Known then" is the snapshot saved with the entry at the replay clock. It never changes after the save.

"What happened after" reads the system operator's notices in the replay, from the entry's time up to the clock, in Manila time.
