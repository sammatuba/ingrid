Sources and method
  • Prices are the region's five-minute median from the WESM replay. Nothing after the replay time is shown.
  • Severity is a stated rule on the recorded price. Extreme is at the offer cap. High is twice the usual price, or longer than 30 minutes.
  • A collapse is high at the offer floor.
  • Usual is the same-hour median of the earlier replay days only. The first day has none.
  • A cause is shown only when a recorded event matches. Otherwise none is claimed.
  • Levels come from the saved alert rules, or the desk's defaults when none are saved.
  • Likely drivers are evidence, not proof. Nothing here is trading advice.
