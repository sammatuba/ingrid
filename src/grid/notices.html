Sources and method
  • NGCP advisories as posted, read from the baked replay. Nothing after the replay time is shown.
  • Times are Manila time. A notice's own time is when it was posted.
  • Technology words in the text are withheld, as on every page.
  • A derate is a notice whose text says derated.
  • A notice names a plant when the event record maps it. Otherwise one plant of that grid must have its code in the text. A notice that fits two or more plants links none.
  • A notice that opened a recorded event links to the event's page.
