InGRID is a review desk.

It reads new evidence, such as an operator notice, and finds the forecast assumption it touches. It asks an AI model fixed questions, then the analyst decides whether to keep or change the assumption.

  1. Open the PEDC Unit 2 trip, a real operator notice of 21 September 2026.
  2. Read what the AI reader answered under the cutoff.
  3. Record your decision, which goes to your own copy.
