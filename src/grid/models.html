Decision model

Built. No Clef call serves the desk.

The decision model returns a typed option distribution for each compiled question.

A choice question carries a choice and probabilities over its options. A yes/no question carries one probability.

The fixture backend carries its own label. It is a test stand-in, not a model run.

Gemini answered the same question set on the real case at two cutoffs, in two recorded runs. They happened on 14 October 2026.

The paper names gemini-3.8-flash, at thinking budget zero. Each compiled question ran in four presentation forms, and the answers held.

The working paper names the class: TypeSafe's Jev, Cloudflare's Clef and OpenAI's Decisions API. Those models return typed option distributions over caller-defined choices.
