Project map

From market signals to accountable decisions. The map joins the theory, the Philippine market data and the domain use cases into one product architecture.

The data, theory and domain workstreams converge. The parts do not yet form a complete decision loop. This page separates what InGRID selected, built, tested and still needs to validate.

InGRID is Interpretable Grid Reasoning for Informed Decisions. It is a review desk for energy analysts. It records each step from evidence to claim to assumption to decision against a grid ontology. Ingrid, its assistant, proposes a position and shows her working. The person decides, and the record shows who decided what on which evidence.

Sam Matuba is the author of the paper. Sam Matuba and Ericka Formanes wrote the codebase. Jayden Cacayan gathered the feedback as the user representative.

01 / THE INTEGRATED MODEL

Three inputs. One accountable workflow.

Data records what happened. Theory governs how evidence influences judgment. Domain expertise names the decisions that matter. InGRID joins all three without making a forecast model the decision maker.

WORKSTREAM B

The theoretical foundation

The core question is “Who answers for a decision made with AI?”

Belief maintenance stays an essential mechanism beneath the wider accountability question.

Evidence, claim, belief, forecast, decision and outcome structure the work.

The design keeps traceability. It leaves consequential decisions with people.

WORKSTREAM C

Domain-specific requirements

The paper collects twelve user stories from the operating company. Record: docs/product/paper-accountable-ai-decisions-2026-10-17.md, as of 2026-10-17.

Those stories and the market factors became the desk questions.

Users include traders, portfolio managers, risk analysts and risk managers.

The factor list is a watch list, not a list of established causes. An unusual signal is evidence. It does not prove why a price moved.

INTEGRATION LAYER

An analyst workspace

The intended workspace combines a grid replay, events, alerts, assumptions, versioned outlooks, portfolio exposure and decisions.

Generative views stay at selected design. Record: docs/work/product-shape-2026-10-17/strategy.md, as of 2026-10-17.

The model would choose fixed, record-backed parts. Code would fill every figure.

The site is not a live, model-generated trading desk.

03 / THE OUTLOOK ARCHIVE

The strongest link between theory and code.

The archive keeps issued versions, input fingerprints, assumption references and information cutoffs. It does not yet show that an analyst revised a forecast because of an attributable event.

ConceptImplemented evidenceStill to demonstrate
EvidenceWESM observations, advisories and source metadata.Bring new grid-constraint signals into adopted readings.
ClaimAttributed structured market findings.Resolve disputed identity and meaning.
BeliefThe assumption register.Owner, validity interval and review discipline.
ForecastImmutable outlook records, fingerprints and cutoffs.A materially revised outlook tied to a reviewed assumption.
DecisionDecision and impact record structures.One real analyst decision with authority and alternatives.
OutcomeScoring and attribution machinery.A settled scored outlook that informs the next decision.

An architecture can keep and test decisions. That is not yet an organisation that used it to learn from decisions.

The archive holds 2 issued outlook versions. The first came on 16 October 2026. The second came after the 17 October data refresh. Record: docs/work/price-outlook/refresh-regeneration-2026-10-17.md, as of 2026-10-17.

Each version names its own information cutoff and input fingerprint. The later version has a newer cutoff. It does not prove that a person changed an assumption.

The PEDC Unit 2 outage has no real dependency link to either version. No settled month has scored either version.

05 / RECOMMENDED NEXT STEPS

Consolidate through an integration contract.

Do not fold all the work into one giant document. Keep the data, theory and domain materials separately maintainable. Join them by one shared definition of what each use case must show.

What did the analyst ask? What evidence existed at the cutoff? Which assumption did the event affect? What forecast and exposure followed? Who authorised the decision? What happened afterwards?

  1. Reconcile the master capability map. Update it from the 17 October work. Show data coverage, readings, code, tests, deployments and open items apart.
  2. Validate one full vertical case. Trace a market event to an actual linked assumption, outlook, exposure and authorised decision. Keep missing links missing.
  3. Keep release scope apart from research scope. Do not raise generative views, live feeds or private portfolio imports above their record. Record the competition artefacts and the deployment state apart.

This path gives a defensible demonstration of the central claim. It does not expand the conceptual scope.

06 / PRODUCT POSITIONING

One project, expressed at three levels.

RESEARCH THESIS

Accountable AI-assisted work

Evidence, uncertainty, authority and outcomes stay distinguishable and reviewable. This holds when people use AI for consequential decisions.

REUSABLE FRAMEWORK

Governed decision records

One record structure joins observations, attributed claims, reviewed assumptions, versioned positions, authorised decisions and later evaluations.

DOMAIN PRODUCT

Electricity-market decision desk

Market professionals can investigate changing conditions, assess exposure, maintain outlooks and defend their judgments. Uncertainty and traceability stay explicit.

VALIDATION TARGET

One reconstructable decision

The next proof is one real decision. Someone can reconstruct its evidence, assumptions, outlook, authorisation and outcome.

The central challenge now is integration, then validation. It is not another wholesale rewrite of the theory.
