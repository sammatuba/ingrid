## Reasons, not numerology

Technical: numerical scripts and responsible evaluation are useful foundations. The current
repository does not report a connected evidence-to-reviewed-outlook app. Gemini must perform
real evidence work, and deployment, persistence, failures and access must be demonstrated.

Impact: the analyst problem is clear, but the real case, manual comparison, adoption evidence
and permitted material remain outstanding. An energy narrative is not an emissions measurement.

Innovation: temporal evidence plus typed judgments plus model-linked revision is a differentiated
hypothesis. A model-name list or decorative graph would weaken it. Show the compiler and one
ablation. Do not claim broad scientific novelty from a combination of known methods.

UX: the existing prototype is monitoring-oriented. The supplied interaction direction makes
evidence, change and review visible; it is not yet the hosted production interface.

## Evidence that would justify the target

A stranger can finish a real, permitted case from source to saved outlook revision without
assistance. The system refuses late evidence and unresolved mappings. A Google model produces
traceable substantive work. Each numerical/S1 artifact has its correct target, provenance and
mode. Matched evaluation and measured task effort are reported honestly. The safe public demo
works without private data or privileged credentials.

85 is an aspirational execution standard, not a forecast. A polished fixture-only presentation
must not be scored as real analyst validation. A model failure should reduce the relevant claim,
not be hidden by a nicer explanation. A missing eligibility item makes the entry not ready,
even when the concept score is high.

## Judge objections to rehearse

'Why not a dashboard?' -> A dashboard shows conditions; this preserves the evidence-to-decision
history and identifies which accepted assumption needs review.

'Why not chat with PDFs?' -> Chat retrieves text; this binds answers to reviewed identity,
known-then versions, model inputs and explicit acceptance transitions.

'Why so many models?' -> They answer different questions; each must beat or complement a baseline.
The workflow remains usable when a model is absent.

'What is actually new?' -> Our proposed compiler and dependency-aware review loop; show tests
rather than a first-ever claim.

'Where is impact?' -> Show the baseline task and actual pilot measures. Wider resilience and
commercial claims are a future validation agenda.
