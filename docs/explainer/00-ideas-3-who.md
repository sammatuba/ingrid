# Who does what

This chapter takes the third question.

## The question

Who may propose, accept, decide and act?

## The idea, in everyday words

Work splits into acts, and the acts do not carry the same authority. One person may prepare a
proposal. Another may accept it. A third may carry it out. The rule holds for machines too: the
model proposes, and a person accepts.

An example. A bank teller looks up an account and proposes a higher transfer limit. The teller
cannot grant it. A manager reads the proposal and accepts or refuses it. The record keeps who
proposed and who decided.

## The desk's word

The desk calls these the roles and the authority, and the record of who proposed and who accepted
is the receipt.

## Where it is thin

The desk's loop stops at acceptance: carrying the work out, with receipts of its own, sits in the
build method and not in the product.
