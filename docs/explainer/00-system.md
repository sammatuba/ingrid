## The foundation, in short

InGRID is a change-review desk for power-system analysts. An operator notice arrives. The
desk asks whether the notice supports or challenges an accepted assumption about the grid, at
the moment the notice became known. Three authorities never mix.

Code owns time, identity, access and the write. Models answer typed questions only inside a
compiled, permitted context. The analyst owns acceptance. The method is a composition of known
parts, and the working paper claims no invention of its own
([working paper](../product/working-paper-2026-10-11.md), sections 1 and 6).

## The systems in place

The map below holds every component that the project uses, in four bands. Each node carries one
of the map's four words: live, built, recorded or designed. The word says how far the component goes
today. The architecture pages use five status words: built on main, running, designed, recorded
once, never run live. Select a node to read what
it is, its role, its status, its numbers and the source record behind it. The coordinator's
inventory record of 15 October 2026 is the source for every node and every word
([inventory](../work/docs-for-people/systems-inventory-2026-10-15.md)).
